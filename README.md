# Mental Health in Technology — EDA Project

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Mental Health in Technology survey dataset to understand mental health treatment patterns among employees working in technology-related workplaces.

The project uses Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) to identify patterns and relationships between mental health treatment and personal, workplace, and organizational factors.

## Problem Statement

Mental health is an important workplace concern. The objective of this project is to explore survey data and understand factors associated with mental health treatment among technology employees.

## Business Objective

The main objectives are to:

- Analyze mental health treatment patterns.
- Study the relationship between family history and treatment.
- Analyze work interference caused by mental health.
- Compare remote and non-remote employees.
- Examine workplace mental health benefits.
- Analyze care options and wellness programs.
- Study employee attitudes toward seeking help.
- Compare treatment patterns across age groups and countries.

## Dataset

The dataset contains survey responses related to mental health in the technology workplace.

The dataset includes information about:

- Age
- Gender
- Country
- Family history
- Mental health treatment
- Work interference
- Remote work
- Workplace benefits
- Care options
- Wellness programs
- Help-seeking support
- Workplace attitudes
- Mental and physical health perceptions
